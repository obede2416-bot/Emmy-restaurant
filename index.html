Who We Are

Our Story

EMMY was born from a passion for extraordinary food and the belief that every meal is an opportunity to create a memory. Founded in 2004 by Chef Emmanuel Rousseau, we have built our reputation on sourcing the finest seasonal ingredients and transforming them into dishes that transcend the ordinary.

Our team of world-class chefs brings decades of Michelin-starred experience to every plate, while our front-of-house team delivers service that is both warm and impeccably professional. At EMMY, dining is not just a meal — it is an experience.

Meet The Artist

Behind The Kitchen

Emmanuel Rousseau

Executive Chef & Founder

Born in Lyon, France, Chef Emmanuel trained at the legendary École Ferrandi Paris before earning his stripes in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe. His philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, honour the season, and cook from the soul.

After two decades of honing his craft across three continents, Emmanuel founded EMMY in 2004 — a restaurant that embodies his belief that the finest dining experiences begin with exceptional produce, honest technique, and unwavering dedication.
